Welcome to PCEA Chuka Town

A home for worship in the heart of Chuka

We are a Presbyterian congregation that gathers each Sunday to hear the Word, pray, sing and serve our neighbours. Whether you have worshipped here for years or are looking for a church family, there is a seat for you.
